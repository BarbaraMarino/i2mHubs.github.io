MODULE 1

Exploring Academic Career Pathways

Estimated Time: 3–5 Hours (Self-Paced)

This module introduces participants to the diversity of faculty careers across higher education. Through videos, graphics, reflections, and interactive activities, participants will explore institutional missions, faculty roles, academic career pathways, the application process, and strategies for navigating academic culture and belonging.

Your Learning Journey

Step 1

Understanding the Academic Landscape

Explore institutional missions, Carnegie classifications, and the wide variety of faculty career environments.

Begin Step 1

Step 2

Faculty Appointments & Academic Rank

Learn about common faculty appointments, promotion pathways, tenure systems, and academic rank.

Step 3

Navigating the Application Process

Learn how faculty positions are advertised and how application materials are tailored to different institutions.

Step 4

Navigating Academic Culture & Belonging

Explore mentoring, belonging, conflict resolution, and strategies for professional success.

Step 5

Final Reflection & Next Steps

Reflect on your learning and identify academic environments that align with your goals and interests.

Looking Ahead

Participants who complete Module 1 will be eligible to apply for the iREDEFINE Workshop, an in-person experience focused on preparing for the academic job search process and exploring pathways to faculty careers.
