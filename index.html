Exploring Academic Career Pathways

An online learning experience designed to help graduate students understand faculty careers, institutional diversity, and the factors that shape academic career decisions.

Begin Module 1

Why This Resource?

Many aspects of academic careers are learned informally through mentoring relationships and personal networks. This platform makes that knowledge more explicit by providing accessible information about faculty roles, institutional missions, and common barriers that may discourage talented individuals from pursuing academic careers.

Faculty Perspectives

Hear directly from faculty members about their career pathways, responsibilities, and experiences across different institution types.

Structured Learning

Learn about teaching, research, service, tenure, promotion, and institutional expectations through curated educational materials.

Guided Reflection

Reflect on your goals, values, interests, and how different academic pathways align with your career aspirations.

The i²mHubs Pathway

Module 1

Build foundational knowledge about academic careers, faculty responsibilities, institutional types, and career alignment.

Module 2

Participate in an in-person workshop focused on preparing for and navigating the academic job search process.

Module 3

Connect with mentoring hubs that provide support during the transition into and through the early years of an academic appointment.

Learning Components

Videos

Faculty interviews and career narratives.

Reading Materials

Explanations of faculty roles, expectations, and institutional contexts.

Reflection Exercises

Activities designed to help participants explore career fit and professional identity.
